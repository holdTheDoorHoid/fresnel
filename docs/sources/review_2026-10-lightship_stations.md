## Conflicts and judgement calls

- **Hog Island Shoal, vessel number.** The 1900 Light List prints "Light-Vessel, No. 12" (page 50, entry 192); the Society's assignment table has LV-22 at the station from 1885 to 1901. The entry is tied by name and year (nothing else in 1900 is called Hog Island Shoal), and its position is used; the number conflict is the Society's or the List's, not settled here.
- **Five Fathom Bank.** Wikidata's item Q18150126 puts the station at 38.8354 N, 75.0119 W, 38 km from the Light List's 38 47 20 N, 74 34 36 W (1900, page 92, entry 462), at the mouth of Delaware Bay rather than off Cape May. The List's point is used and Wikidata's is set aside (`record_joins.py`).
- **Southeast Shoal: Canada (Ontario), decided.** The Great Lakes list of 1925 prints a lightship at Southeast Shoal, Lake Erie, at 41 49 21 N, 82 27 32 W (page 72, entry 362). The Society files the station under Ohio, years 1901-1910 (a vessel of the Lake Carriers' Association), but its own location field reads "Lake Erie in Canadian Waters". Evidence for Ontario over Ohio: the point lies inside Ontario's boundary polygon (Natural Earth) and outside Ohio's, which is found only by probing 20 km out; the page image shows the entry printed under the heading "LAKE ERIE, NORTH SIDE", after Port Burwell, Port Stanley and Rondeau Harbour, all Ontario ports; and the Canadian Southeast Shoal Lighthouse (`ca-on-southeast-shoal`, OpenStreetMap 41.8263 N, 82.4628 W) stands 0.6 km from it. The entry is tied by hand (`ENTRY_STATIONS`) and the station is filed under Canada, Ontario (`COUNTRY_OF_POSITION`), as `ca-on-southeast-shoal-2`. Not linked as the lighthouse's predecessor: nothing read says the lighthouse replaced the lightship.
- **Swiftsure (LV-83): one station, decided.** The map held two entries for the surviving lightship: `us-wa-swiftsure` (Wikidata Q7656131, 47.6749 N, 122.207 W) and `us-wa-swiftsure-2` (Lighthouse Friends 185 and National Register 75001852). They are one vessel: both carry NRHP 75001852; Wikidata's article is "United States lightship Swiftsure (LV-83)" and Lighthouse Friends' title is "Lightship Swiftsure LV 83/WAL 508"; both are in King County. The matcher missed it because Wikidata's point is 11.0 km from the register's batch point (47.6282, -122.3367) and Lighthouse Friends' (47.62806, -122.33703), which agree with each other to 30 m at Lake Union, Seattle, past the 10 km hard-key radius. `record_joins.py` joins the two records of `-2` into `us-wa-swiftsure` and sets Wikidata's point aside; `us-wa-swiftsure-2` is retired (`merged_into`, file kept, address redirects). The vessel record LV-83 berths at `us-wa-swiftsure`.
- **LV-75.** The Society lists LV-75 as existing and in the same remark says it was possibly scrapped in 2013: the vessel record has `doubtful: true` and no berth, and nothing is added to the map for it.
- **Not moorings.** Curtis Bay Depot, Staten Island Depot (depots where lightships were kept), Ohio Ledge (planned, never built) and Humboldt Bay Bell Boat (a bell boat) are on the list the Society keeps; they are kept here with the reason and have no position to find.
