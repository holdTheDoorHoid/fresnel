# Fresnel

**Every lighthouse in the United States (territories included) and Canada on one map:
standing or lost, with the lightships, pier and range lights and life-saving stations beside
them, how to visit, and the long history of each place and the people who kept its light.**

Sister site to [Firefinder](https://github.com/holdTheDoorHoid/firefinder), which does the same
for fire lookouts. Named for the Fresnel lens at the heart of nearly every lighthouse.

> Status: just started (2026-10-21). Entries marked **Unverified** come from a single source and
> have not been checked yet. Corrections are very welcome.

## What each station page will have

- **Visit & stay**: grounds, tower climbs, museums, boat-only access, rentals, inns and
  volunteer keeper programs.
- **History, in chapters**: the place, building the light, the keepers (with a roster of names
  and years), storms, wrecks and rescues, what came after the keepers, and today. Every fact is
  footnoted.
- **The light at night**: the light as a sailor saw it, flashing in its real pattern, and how
  far out it could be seen.
- **Keepers index**: every keeper linked across the stations they served.

## Corrections, additions, takedowns

- Use **Suggest an edit** on any station page, or [open an issue](https://github.com/holdTheDoorHoid/fresnel/issues/new).
- **Photo or text removal:** if you own something shown here and want it credited differently
  or removed, open an issue titled "Takedown: <station name>". It will be handled promptly, no
  questions asked.
