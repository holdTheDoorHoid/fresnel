Fresnel is an open map of every lighthouse in the United States (its territories included) and Canada, standing or lost, with the lightships, the pier, harbor and range lights and the life-saving stations beside them. It is named for the Fresnel lens, the stack of glass prisms at the heart of nearly every lighthouse.

Sister site: Firefinder, every fire lookout in the US and Canada.
