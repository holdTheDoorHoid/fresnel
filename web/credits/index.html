Sources and credits

Where Fresnel’s facts come from

Fresnel takes facts (positions, dates, heights, light patterns, keeper names and years, register numbers) from the sources below, credits them on every station page that uses them, and writes its own words. Their text is never copied.

Histories and keepers

Keeper names, roles and years in the keepers index, and the roster of any station whose keepers have not yet been researched, come from the U.S. Lighthouse Society’s Digital Archive (© U.S. Lighthouse Society, all rights reserved), which transcribes the National Archives’ keeper registers. Only facts are taken (names, roles, years, places): its text is not copied. Each roster and each person’s page links to the archive’s own pages.

The histories and keeper rosters cite their own sources, footnote by footnote and line by line, on each station’s page: annual reports of the US Lighthouse Board (1852–1910), the Commissioner of Lighthouses (1911–1939) and the US Life-Saving Service (1876–1914); the Canadian Department of Marine and Fisheries reports in the Sessional Papers; keeper registers; National Register and Canadian heritage nominations; and newspapers of the time.
