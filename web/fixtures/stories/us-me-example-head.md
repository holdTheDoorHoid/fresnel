## The place

Example Head is a granite point at the mouth of an invented harbor, used here only to test how a story renders.[^1]

## Building the light

The first tower was lit in 1791 and replaced by a brick tower in 1855.[^1][^2]

## The keepers

On most stations of the period, keepers trimmed the wicks every few hours through the night.[^3] At Example Head, the first keeper served for four years.[^2]

## Today

The tower is open on summer weekends.[^1]
