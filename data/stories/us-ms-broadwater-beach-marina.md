Broadwater Beach Marina Light is a white light on a cylindrical tower that marks the entrance to a marina on Beach Boulevard (US 90) in Biloxi, Mississippi, about four miles (6 km) west of the historic Biloxi Light.[^1][^2] The tower is about 66 feet (20 m) tall, with a gallery and an open lantern, and it flashes white every five seconds.[^1]

## Building the light

Every source that gives a year says 1965. The Lighthouse Directory and the Lighthouse Hunters site both print it with a question mark, and neither says where the date comes from.[^1][^4] A Flickr photograph taken in late October 2006 has a caption saying the light was privately built in 1965 at the marina for the Broadwater Beach Hotel.[^3] James Johnston, who visited in 2023, says it was built in 1965 for the Broadwater Beach Resort and marks the marina entrance.[^2] The available sources do not say when the light was first lit, or who maintained it before the 2005 hurricane.

## Storms, wrecks and rescues

The accounts of the 1969 storm differ. Johnston says Hurricane Camille devastated the Mississippi coast that year. He reports that the resort was badly damaged but rebounded, and that the light survived with only minor damage.[^2] The Lighthouse Hunters site says the resort was destroyed by Camille in 1969, and says nothing about the light's condition then.[^4]

Hurricane Katrina struck on 25 August 2005. The photographer's Flickr caption says it destroyed most of Biloxi, including the hotel and the marina, but that the light survived with minor damage.[^3] A March 2007 photograph shows the light still standing after the marina it marked had vanished.[^1]

## After the keepers

In June 2020 Dakia Entertainment Hospitality and Broadwater Development, LLP signed a letter of intent to redevelop the former hotel, marina and golf course as a $1.2 billion, 266-acre entertainment complex, WLOX reported.[^5] The Lighthouse Directory gives the figure as $12 billion, which the news report does not support. The directory also says that as of 2022 no construction was in progress, and it names Broadwater Development LLC as the owner, operator and site manager.[^1]

## Today

The Lighthouse Directory says the light is still listed on the Coast Guard's Light List. It describes the light as privately maintained and apparently active, with the site open and the tower closed.[^1] The Society's record calls it a minor aid to navigation, says it is not open to the public, and names no keepers.[^6] The two sources disagree on access.

When Johnston visited in 2023 he found the tower covered in graffiti and the pier railing rusty, though the pier and the base looked stable. He wrote that the 2020 renderings still show the lighthouse.[^2] The available sources do not say what the marina looks like today.
